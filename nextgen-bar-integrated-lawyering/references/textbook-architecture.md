# `OPT-66-NEXTGEN-BAR` textbook-skill architecture

**Architecture ID:** `F2-ARCH-66-NEXTGEN-BAR-001`  
**Status:** `SPEC_DRAFT`  
**Promise:** Teach integrated lawyering reasoning as source-grounded, audience-aware, jurisdiction-bounded professional work.

## Learner change

Before: the learner gives a legal-sounding answer or lists doctrine.  
After: the learner frames the matter, locates authority, analyzes facts and issues, offers options, communicates uncertainty, and escalates when the task exceeds scope.

## Progression

`Matter → Authority → Facts and issues → Application → Options → Communication → Review / escalation`

## Unit map

| Unit | Work | Learner evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client, matter, purpose, jurisdiction, and confidentiality | matter-orientation record |
| 2 | Legal research and authority control | authority log and source hierarchy |
| 3 | Issue spotting, investigation, and fact evaluation | issue tree and fact ledger |
| 4 | Rule / fact application and options | analysis memo and options matrix |
| 5 | Counseling, negotiation, and dispute resolution | client-advice or negotiation plan |
| 6 | Professional writing, review, and handoff | final work product plus escalation record |

## Human-plus-agent design

The agent may organize supplied authority, identify open issues, draft alternative structures, and test completeness. It may not silently invent law, choose jurisdiction, give live legal advice, or replace qualified review. The human confirms authority, facts, scope, professional responsibility, and final communication.

## Evaluator seed

Dimensions: authority fidelity, issue coverage, factual grounding, rule / fact reasoning, options and consequences, audience fit, uncertainty, jurisdiction, confidentiality, and escalation. A polished memo with unsupported law fails.

## First drafting packet

Draft `BAR-001` will use a fictional, clearly labeled matter with a supplied packet of public legal materials. The task will require a source log, issue tree, client-facing options memo, and a red-team review for jurisdiction drift.
