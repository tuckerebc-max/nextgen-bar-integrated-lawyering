## Source-grounded construct

**SOURCE:** NCBE's NextGen UBE content scope identifies foundational lawyering skills: legal research, legal writing, issue spotting and analysis, investigation and evaluation, client counseling and advising, negotiation and dispute resolution, and client relationship and management. It states that legal resources may be provided so examinees can demonstrate skills rather than rely on a complete base of knowledge for every contextual area.

## Assessment-versus-competency boundary

**SOURCE:** The NextGen UBE is a licensure assessment with a current, jurisdiction-sensitive content scope.  
**INFERENCE:** The textbook competency is integrated lawyering under constraints: frame a problem, research supplied or authoritative law, analyze facts and rules, communicate options, advise within role, negotiate or resolve where appropriate, and document uncertainty and escalation.  
**NOT CLAIMED:** Passing the exam equals independent legal practice, jurisdictional authorization, or accurate advice in a live matter.

## Construct decomposition

1. Clarify client, legal, factual, procedural, and professional purpose.
2. Locate and evaluate governing authority and provided resources.
3. Separate facts, assumptions, unknowns, issues, rules, and implications.
4. Apply authority to facts and identify counterarguments.
5. Generate options with consequences, uncertainty, and next evidence needs.
6. Communicate in the genre and register required by the professional task.
7. Protect confidentiality, scope, professional responsibility, and escalation.

## Evidence and task model

**CROSSWALK:** Core task families are legal research packet, issue-analysis memo, investigation plan, client-advice record, negotiation / dispute-resolution plan, and relationship / handoff record.  
**EVALUATOR DESIGN:** Require a source log, issue tree, factual record, rule-application chain, options matrix, uncertainty statement, and jurisdiction check. Score fidelity to sources, issue coverage, reasoning, audience fit, and safe escalation.

## Textbook spine

`Matter orientation → Authority and fact record → Issue analysis → Options and advice → Negotiation / resolution → Professional writing → Review, escalation, and handoff`

## Failure modes and constraints

- hallucinated law or uncited authority;
- jurisdiction drift;
- legal conclusion stated without facts or rule application;
- advice that hides uncertainty or conflicts;
- confidentiality breach;
- agent output presented as counsel;
- reproduction of protected NCBE materials.
