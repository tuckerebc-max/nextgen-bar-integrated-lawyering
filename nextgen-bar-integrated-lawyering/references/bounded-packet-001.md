## `OPT-66-NEXTGEN-BAR` — Authority, facts, issues, and options

**Learner target:** Produce a bounded professional reasoning record from supplied fictional materials.

**Fictional packet:** A city rule excerpt, a fictional timeline, a client objective, and two conflicting factual statements. The packet is educational and not a real jurisdiction or legal matter.

**Learner task:** Identify the role, purpose, authority, facts, unknowns, issues, and possible next steps. Draft a short options memo that labels assumptions and requests professional review where the fictional packet is insufficient.

**Required artifact:** authority log, fact / issue ledger, rule-fact analysis, options matrix, uncertainty and escalation record.

**Nonmodel:** A confident legal conclusion with no authority locator or jurisdiction statement.  
**Feedback move:** Ask “Which source controls this statement, and what would change if the missing fact were different?”

**Agent handoff:** Agent may organize supplied materials and draft competing structures; human reviewer confirms authority, scope, and final language.

**Exit test:** Add a second jurisdiction label that conflicts with the first. The learner must stop and request clarification rather than choose silently.
