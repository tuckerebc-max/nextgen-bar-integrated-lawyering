## `OPT-66-NEXTGEN-BAR` evaluator

**Primary evidence:** matter record, authority log, fact / issue ledger, rule-fact analysis, options memo, review / escalation record.

**Dimensions:** authority fidelity; jurisdiction; issue and fact coverage; rule-fact reasoning; options / consequences; audience fit; uncertainty; confidentiality; escalation.

**Critical failures:** invented law, wrong jurisdiction, confidentiality breach, advice outside scope, or unsupported legal conclusion.

**Challenge set design:** supplied public authorities; incomplete facts; conflicting authority; client-facing communication; negotiation / dispute-resolution scenario; deliberate jurisdiction mismatch.

**Improvement loop:** diagnose authority or scope failure → revise source-control and jurisdiction instructions → rerun with an unseen fictional matter → qualified reviewer checks legal fidelity.
